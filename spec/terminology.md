# Terminology

**Case** — one bounded assurance engagement.

**Evidence cutoff** — the declared boundary after which new evidence is not silently treated as contemporaneous to the evaluated action.

**Evidence root** — cryptographic identity of the exact evidence bundle provided to evaluators.

**Case root** — cryptographic identity of the complete case package, including metadata, determinations, comparison and attestation.

**Evaluator** — an independently operating assessment participant. Evaluator independence is procedural unless organizational independence is explicitly stated.

**Frozen determination** — immutable evaluator result bound to case, evidence root, method identity/version and freeze time.

**Reveal** — the first point at which evaluators may inspect each other's frozen determinations.

**Comparison** — post-reveal artifact recording convergence, evaluator-specific findings, disagreement and unresolved boundaries.

**Representation confirmation** — each evaluator confirms that the comparison fairly represents its own frozen finding.

**Joint attestation** — final artifact acknowledging the comparison and its integrity; it does not imply agreement on every finding.

**Evidentiary-boundary disagreement** — disagreement about what evidence existed, was reachable, timely, accessible, trustworthy or legitimately usable at the relevant action boundary.

# Evaluator independence

The protocol protects **procedural independence**.

Before reveal, the orchestrator MUST prevent:
- access to another evaluator's determination;
- prompts that summarize another evaluator's conclusion;
- shared post-reveal taxonomy being injected as a required native reasoning model;
- comparison drafts being shown before the evaluator has frozen its own result.

Organizational, financial or methodological independence MUST be described separately and only when factual.

The orchestrator MAY know evaluator identities and routing metadata but SHOULD minimize access to proprietary evaluator reasoning beyond the frozen artifact necessary for comparison.

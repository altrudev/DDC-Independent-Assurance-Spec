# Comparison

The comparison layer is post-reveal.

A comparison record SHOULD preserve native evaluator wording separately from the shared comparison summary.

Required categories:
- convergence;
- evaluator-specific finding;
- conclusion disagreement;
- evidentiary-boundary disagreement;
- unresolved;
- out of scope.

Neither evaluator MAY modify the other's frozen determination. Characterization of an evaluator's finding MUST be confirmed by that evaluator before the comparison is final.
